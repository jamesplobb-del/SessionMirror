# BestTake Plus

Product decision, 16 September 2026. This is the free/paid boundary until it is replaced in writing.

**BestTake Plus — $9.99 once.** The app is free to download. One unlock. Family Sharing on. No subscription.

Promo-code and StoreKit wiring live in `PROMO_CODES.md`. That document does not change these boundaries.

## Always free

- Tuner (live pitch, transpose, needle)
- Metronome
- Drone
- Record audio or video
- Playback, best take, split/compare
- Hands-free record
- Learn game
- Open a routine someone shared with you
- Share a routine
- Games’ basic play (Staff Jumper free set below; Learn fully free)

## Free with a cap

- **Multitrack:** up to 4 boxes
- **Routines:** 3 saved (shared ones still open)
- **Take vault:** 30 takes (delete to make room; never cut off a take in progress)

## Paid (the $9.99 unlock)

- Pitch Insights (history, tendencies, over-time tracking)
- Multitrack 5–12 boxes + extra boxes
- Unlimited routines
- Unlimited vault
- Staff Jumper depth (below)

Pitch Insights still records in the background on free. Paying unlocks the screen, with history already there.

## Staff Jumper

**Free**

- Play the game
- Major keys: C, G, F, D, Bb, Eb
- 1 octave
- Easy and medium

**Paid**

- Other major keys
- Minor keys
- Custom / build-your-own scale
- 2-octave range
- Hard

Learn stays fully free. Do not add a second paywall on Balance or characters.

## Hit a cap

They keep what they have. They can delete. One upgrade prompt. No mid-take stop. No auto-delete.

## Do not lock

- Compare
- Recording
- Live tuner
- Hands-free
- Opening a shared routine
