# The Little Ensemble

Final artwork: editable SVG extensions of the existing 96×112 game characters.
Flat fills, 3–4px outlines, simple expressions, original small-sprite proportions.
The generated 2.5D concepts were rejected in favor of this simpler direction;
none of that generated artwork is used by the app.

- Otto — trumpet otter. A cheerful warm-up partner; starts unlocked.
- Cleo — violin cat. Original purple cat with a violin and bow; 3 trophies.
- Riff — drummer raccoon. A waist-high snare and two sticks; 7 trophies.
- Pip — flute bird. Original blue bird with a silver flute; 12 trophies.
- Reed — saxophone fox. Original fox with a compact gold sax; 20 trophies.
- Luna — conductor owl. Two raised wings and a baton; 30 trophies.

All characters use the original transparent canvas and shared baseline. Original
storage IDs remain unchanged for saved selections and existing Balance rewards.
The source files in this directory are the production artwork for both games,
the games lobby, Home's next-unlock preview, and the trophy collection.
