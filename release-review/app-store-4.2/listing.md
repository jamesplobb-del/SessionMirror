## description

Practice with your sheet music, tuner, metronome, and recordings on the same screen. Open a PDF, move the tools where you want them, and record without leaving the page.

You know the advice: record yourself and listen back. BestTake keeps your takes together so you can compare performances, mark your favorite, and hear what changed.

YOUR MUSIC AND TOOLS, TOGETHER
Bring a PDF or photo into the Music Stand. Add markings, play along with backing audio, or keep a YouTube reference close by. Keep your practice tools open over your sheet music or recording screen. Music features are included with Plus.

KEEP YOUR INSTRUMENT IN YOUR HANDS
With BestTake Plus, hands-free recording listens for you to start playing, records your take, plays it back, and gets ready for the next one.

RECORD, LISTEN, TRY AGAIN
Record audio or video, leave notes on a take, and share a recording with your teacher or a friend. Keep as many takes as your device has room for. No account to create.

BUILD YOUR PRACTICE ROUTINE
Save the exercises you want to work on. Each exercise opens the tools it needs. One saved routine is free; Plus adds unlimited saved routines.

TUNER, METRONOME, AND DRONE
A live tuner with transposition, a metronome with subdivisions, and a tuning drone are always free.

PLAY EVERY PART YOURSELF
Record each part in multitrack, then hear them together. Use two boxes for free, or up to twelve with Plus.

A FUN WAY TO PRACTICE
Play the Learn and Balance games with your instrument. Try Staff Jumper on Easy for free, then move to Medium and Hard with Plus.

TRACK YOUR PITCH OVER TIME
Pitch Insights in Plus keeps a history of the notes you hold, so you can see which notes tend sharp or flat and how your playing changes over time.

MOST TOOLS ARE FREE. PLUS IS A ONE-TIME PURCHASE.
Recording, playback, sharing, the tuner, metronome, drone, one saved routine, two multitrack boxes, Learn, Balance, and Easy Staff Jumper are free.

BestTake Plus adds hands-free recording, sheet music, backing tracks, YouTube references, Pitch Insights, unlimited saved routines, more multitrack boxes, and the remaining Staff Jumper levels. An optional seven-day trial is available to eligible users. It does not renew or charge automatically.

Your recordings stay on your device. BestTake does not upload them.

Made by a solo developer, with plenty more to come. Found a bug or have an idea? There is a report link in Settings. I would love to hear it.

## reviewNotes

BestTake is a practice recording app for musicians. No account or sign-in is required.

To test recording, allow microphone access, choose audio mode, record a few seconds, and stop. The take appears below the record control. Record a second take to compare them. Camera access is only needed for video recording. The tuner and instrument-controlled games also need microphone input.

BestTake Plus is an optional non-consumable purchase (com.besttake.app.plus.lifetime). The optional seven-day trial is a separate $0 non-consumable (com.besttake.app.plus.trial7days); it never renews or charges automatically. Plus includes hands-free recording, imported sheet music and backing audio, YouTube references, Pitch Insights, additional routines and multitrack boxes, and Medium/Hard Staff Jumper.

Learn, Balance, Easy Staff Jumper, manual recording, playback/export, the live tuner, metronome, drone, one saved routine, and two multitrack boxes work without Plus. YouTube references use YouTube's embedded player.

Build 22 removes all custom access-code entry and redemption, including sandbox/TestFlight and development unlocks. Stored code flags no longer grant Plus. Only verified StoreKit purchases or the verified free trial grant access. The App Store offer-code button opens Apple's redemption sheet and does not grant access on its own.

Purchase testing: Settings > BestTake Plus > Explore BestTake Plus. Use Unlock forever for the lifetime purchase, Try Plus free for 7 days for the free trial, or Restore purchases to restore verified Apple transactions. There are no auto-renewing subscriptions in this binary. Both product IDs above must be included for review with this build.
