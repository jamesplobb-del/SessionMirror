# BestTake visual concepts — generation prompts

Built-in Image Generation tool. Approval-only concept boards, not app implementation.

## A · Studio

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished, high-fidelity visual concept board for BestTake, an existing iPhone music practice app. This is a design proposal for owner approval, not a screenshot of implemented software. Landscape board, ideally 3840x2160. Five equal, tall front-facing screen mockups in one evenly spaced row with readable crisp interface typography. Minimal neutral presentation background, no perspective, no hands, no devices tilted, no decorative props. Small board title at top left; beneath it tiny caption 'Visual direction • concept only'. Each screen has identical quiet iPhone status and home-indicator safe areas, and a consistent bottom navigation with Home, Practice, Takes, Games. No overlapping floating widgets. Each screen may show its own active nav item. Consistent header position, control shapes, text hierarchy, icon stroke, spacing and accent meanings across ALL five screens. Use lots of breathing room, clear musical interfaces, no wordy paragraphs and no generic analytics dashboard. Keep blue action and gold best-take/achievement identity. Fine separators instead of boxes around everything.

SCREENS left to right, captions beneath screens 'Home', 'Record', 'Tuner', 'Routines', 'Games':
HOME: title 'BestTake', small 'Trumpet' instrument selector, heading 'Ready when you are.' then a strong 'Quick Practice' action, a simple 'My Routines' row. Today's routine 'Morning essentials', '2 of 4 complete', tasteful thin progress indicator and 'Continue'. Below a small secondary 'Today’s challenge' with 'One good minute' and tiny gold trophy.
RECORD: title 'Practice', segmented control 'Record  Tuner  Metronome'. Main spacious waveform with time '00:24', clear red round record/stop control. Below, 'Latest take' with play and 'Keep best' / 'Try again' actions. Small 'Reference' row with play. Clear restraint, no input settings clutter. Bottom nav consistent.
TUNER: title 'Practice', same segmented 'Record  Tuner  Metronome', Tuner selected. Large musical 'B♭' with octave '4', small 'In tune', accurate supporting frequency '466.2 Hz'. A refined simple cents curve and centered indicator, contained green status, never green whole screen. Three quiet controls Drone, Metronome, Settings. Compact routine progress strip in reserved space with 'Long tones' and Pause / Next; must not overlap navigation.
ROUTINES: title 'My Routines', 'Morning essentials', '20 min · Trumpet'. Four clean exercise rows 'Breathing' 'Long tones' 'Lip slurs' 'Repertoire' with durations and small tool hints. A strong 'Start routine' action and modest 'Edit' action. No instructions or huge setup steps.
GAMES: intentionally playful arcade world inside the same application navigation, with a charming small illustrated trumpet-playing otter, musical staff platforms and gold coins or trophies. This screen MUST remain distinctly arcade-like and appealing, not a sterile settings list. Title 'Games'; 'Choose your challenge'. Featured 'Staff Jumper' with a playful illustrated staff/platform scene and clear Play button. Secondary smaller 'Balance' and 'Learn' choices, compact character/avatar picker and '3 trophies'. Arcade content may be colorful and dimensional; header, standard type, navigation, outer margins and control discipline stay related to the other four screens. No overcrowded characters or long descriptions. Do not add AI chat, subscriptions, social feeds, scores about musical ability, extra functionality or new logos. Avoid dense card grids, neon gradients and decorative mockup annotations.
BOARD TITLE: 'A · Studio'.
DIRECTION: Recommended direction. Premium calm midnight-navy music utility. All five screens share deep ink/navy background (#0d1727), soft slate content surfaces, crisp near-white system-style type, bright blue actions, warm gold best-take marks. Modest translucent navigation, almost no decorative blur. Game play field is a contained colorful sky-blue arcade scene within the same navy app shell, with warm gold raised Play button. Game art retains friendliness without changing the whole app into a cartoon. Mature musical clarity, understated craftsmanship.
