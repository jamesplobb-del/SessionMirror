# Additional arcade concepts
Preview-only skins, layered over Electric. Arcade Pop uses mint/cream, outlined cards and tactile gold buttons. Midnight Arcade uses navy, cyan and gold with light music-reading surfaces. Gallery buttons switch the right panel without navigation or resetting the screen. These are game skins; other app screens retain Electric.

Source: arcade-style-concepts.css. Import after electric-refinement.css. preview-entry.ts includes the validated arcadeStyle message field. refinement-review.html contains the picker controls and descriptions.
