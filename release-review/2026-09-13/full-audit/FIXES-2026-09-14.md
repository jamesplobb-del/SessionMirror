# Routine fixes — September 14, 2026

Implemented the user's requested fixes for A01, A02 and A03, plus an X to end the active routine session.

- Routine bar now shows the attached program's name and Play/Pause control. This remains visible in the compact bar. The folded ball opens the bar as before.
- X stops routine timing, exits its program, stops metronome/drone playback, clears the active practice context and closes the widget. Saved routines and completed progress remain; closing does not mark an unfinished item complete.
- Section cards capture the pointer only after a swipe is recognized. Ordinary Edit/title/action clicks keep their correct targets; clicks following a swipe are suppressed.
- Both imported-file parsing and normal-save normalization now bound tempo pins against the actual pattern length. Removing the premature normal-save clamp was necessary as well as fixing the importer.

Validation:

- TypeScript compilation passed.
- Routine sharing regression checks passed, including standalone and embedded programs with cycle-based patterns, repeated patterns and total-measure patterns. Bar/beat/BPM pins at bars 12, 28 and 18 survive the relevant round trips.
- Program playback, routine lifecycle, background metronome and focused-practice verification scripts passed.
- Browser preview: restored Play changed to Pause and started the metronome; X removed the routine bar and returned the metronome to stopped; an ordinary Edit click opened the section; moving a tempo pin to bar 12, saving and reopening retained bar 12.

This does not certify iPhone touch gestures, every routine interaction, or native export. Multitrack was not modified in this change. Previously corrupted saved tempo pins cannot be reconstructed automatically; correct them manually or reimport an original file that still has the intended values.
