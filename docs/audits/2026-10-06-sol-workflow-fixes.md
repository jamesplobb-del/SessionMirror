The strongest fresh challenge found that clearing annotations after saving was insufficient: a failing removal could still display A's markings on B. The first candidate was revised to bind persisted ink to the committed score identity, and the native/web failure cases passed. A further delayed-read test reproduced old A overwriting the cache after B committed; the loader now checks the committed pointer after asynchronous reads. Captured-identity reader writes reject stale ownership.
