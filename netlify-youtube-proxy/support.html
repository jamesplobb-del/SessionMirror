BestTake Support

Need help with a take, a practice tool, or BestTake Plus?

Email BestTakeSupport@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, BestTake version, and what happened. Do not send a recording unless you want us to review it.

Quick checks

If recording or pitch detection does not start, check BestTake’s microphone permission in iOS Settings. Video recording also needs camera permission. The metronome and other features that do not use those permissions remain available.

If BestTake Plus does not appear after a purchase, open the Plus screen and choose Restore purchases while signed in with the Apple Account used to buy it.

You can also open BestTake’s in-app Help and Settings for feature instructions and permissions.
